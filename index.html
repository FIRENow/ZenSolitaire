♠ Flipflop Spades

How to play Build each foundation (top left) from Ace up to King. In the lanes, place a card on one rank higher or lower; runs of one suit that go steadily up or down move together, a lane's face-up cards of a single suit also move together even when they zigzag (like 7-6-5-6-5-6), from any card in the pile. Any card can go in an empty lane, and a lane holds at most 20 cards when you move (a deal always adds one). Foundation tops can be pulled back down. Reserve cards (top right) can be played into the game but never returned. Tap the stock to deal one card onto each lane. Drag a card to where it should go, or tap it and then tap the destination (valid spots get a green outline). Tap a selected card again to send it to a foundation.
